About

What is Let's Build?

Daniel Rowland on the vision behind the Audiotool Hackathon Series: what we're building, who it's for, and why now.

Daniel Rowland / LANDR Audio, Professor MTSUOpen on YouTube
Directions

Build your idea

Four directions to get inspired. Pick one, or stretch across several.

01

Creation

Instruments, sequencers, and tools that shape how music is made.

02

Games

Gamified approaches to making, experiencing, and learning music and sound.

03

Listening

Players, visualizers, remix tools, and apps that reimagine how we hear music.

04

Education

Tools for learning music, theory, and production by doing.

When you submit your app, our panel of judges evaluates which categories it fits and tags it accordingly. A single project can qualify and win in multiple categories.

Who should join

No gatekeeping

Every background is welcome, and the best teams mix them.

Musicians & Producers

AI coding turns ideas into apps quickly. Alongside developers and AI models, your vision becomes reality. No prior coding required.

Developers

Go from prototype to production-ready with AI-assisted coding. Work with musicians who bring domain expertise, and build apps creators want.

Students

Learn by building. Explore music tech, work in teams, and add a real project to your portfolio, with free platform access and support from the Audiotool team.

Designers

Shape how people experience music apps. Interfaces, branding, and interaction design are key to every winning project.

Music enthusiasts, creatives & builders

Into production, gaming, or just love music? If you like to create and tinker, the Series is for you. Bring your curiosity and build something new.
